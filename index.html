LEAPFALL

Pull back, release, leap! Climb a deadly waterfall

You are a determined little frog. Aim with one finger, let go, and stick the landing on the platforms above. Dodge mobs, collect flies and stay ahead of the rising water.

How it plays

One thumb. One frog. A long way up.

  1. Pull back to aim and set your power.
  2. Release to launch.
  3. Leap from platform to platform and keep climbing the falls.

In the game

Straight from the waterfall

  • Daily Challenge

    A fresh climb every day.

  • Weekly Tournament

    A week to set your best.

  • Groups

    Compete with your own crew.
