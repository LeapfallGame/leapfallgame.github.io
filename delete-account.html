Delete your Leapfall account

Leapfall is published by LEAP FALL LTD. You can ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

Email hello@leapfall.com with the subject "Delete my Leapfall account".

Please include your Leapfall username so we can find your account.

We will confirm by email and delete your data within 30 days.

What we delete

  • Your username
  • Your anonymous player ID
  • Your scores and leaderboard entries
  • Your group memberships
  • Your Daily and Weekly competition entries and prize records
  • Your coins, skins and other in-game progress stored on our server

What we keep

We do not keep any of the data listed above once it is deleted.

Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is managed by Google under its own privacy policy. You can reset your device's advertising ID in your phone's settings.

More information

See our Privacy Policy for full details of the data we collect and how we use it.
